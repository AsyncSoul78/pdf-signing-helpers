# About Sign PDF

Most sites that sign PDFs start with a routine: make a signature in a separate window, choose a style, save it, and only then drag it onto the document. [Toolster Sign PDF](https://toolster.net/sign_pdf) skips all of that. You open the file and the pen is already in your hand, so you sign on the line the same way you would sign a printout.

## Signing like on paper

The pen behaves like an ordinary ballpoint: thin dark blue ink, with black one click away. If the mouse makes the line shaky, smoothing evens out the tremor and the signature still looks like your own hand.

What ends up in the file is your handwriting on the page, the same as signing a printout and scanning it back. Nothing hidden is added to the PDF. That is what most everyday papers need: a lease, a school form, an application, an invoice. If an organization asks you to sign inside its own service or by an official procedure, use what they offer.

## Text and dates

Switch to **Text**, click the spot and type your name, a title or a short note. Right-click the page and **Today's date** puts the date right where you clicked.

## Quick insert

Every signature and every piece of text you make is kept on its own. Next time it waits in the **Quick insert** row: drag it onto the page, click it and then the spot, or pick it from the right-click menu. Signatures stay in this browser on this device only and disappear if you clear the site data. The small cross on a saved item removes it.

## Adjusting and repeating

Click a signature or a text to select it. Drag it, make it bigger or smaller, change its color, or repeat it in the same place on every page, which saves time on long agreements with initials on each sheet.

## On a phone

Tap the spot where the signature goes: a frame appears and the page zooms in. Sign inside it with your finger and tap Done. Or hold your finger on the page and choose **Draw a signature** to sign in a large box.

## Saving

**Save PDF** downloads the signed copy with `-signed` added to the name. The original pages are kept as they were, so the text stays selectable and links and bookmarks still work.

## Privacy

The PDF is opened and saved on your device and is never uploaded. There is no account and no sign-up.

## Limits

Password protected PDFs do not open yet. Remove the password in the program that set it and open the file again.
